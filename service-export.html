Global Export & Trade Logistics

Seamless ocean container and air freight export handling from Colombo Port directly to 38+ countries with complete customs and phytosanitary clearance.

Inquire for Export Freight

Compliant Documentation, Global Transit Purity

International agricultural customs requirements are rigorous. Failure to maintain correct phytosanitary declarations, non-GMO endorsements, or organic transaction certificates can lead to costly port demurrage and delayed shipments.

COCONEST's experienced in-house export division oversees the entire documentation suite: Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (GSP Form A), USDA NOP and EU Bio Transaction Certificates, Phytosanitary Inspection Reports, and independent SGS third-party pre-shipment inspections.

Incoterms Supported

FOB Colombo

Loaded on vessel at Port of Colombo with export customs and terminal charges settled.

CIF Major Ports

Ocean maritime freight and comprehensive marine transit insurance included to your seaport.

DDP Door Delivery

Turnkey delivery including destination customs clearance and inland truck freight to warehouse.
