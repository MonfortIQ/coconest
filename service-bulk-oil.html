Bulk Virgin Coconut Oil Supply

Supplying multinational cosmetics formulators, therapeutic skincare brands, dietary supplement encapsulators, and commercial organic kitchens in 20L, 200L, and 1,000L formats.

Request Bulk Oil Spec Sheet

Pharmaceutical Grade Purity, Uninterrupted Supply

For cosmetics laboratories and food manufacturers, fatty acid stability and low moisture are vital. High water content in bulk oils leads to hydrolysis, elevating free fatty acids and creating foul rancidity inside industrial tanks.

COCONEST bulk virgin coconut oil guarantees moisture content below 0.03% and peroxide values under 0.2 meq/kg. Every drum is nitrogen-flushed prior to sealing to prevent photo-oxidation and ensure a verified 24-month unopened shelf life.
