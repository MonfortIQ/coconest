Why Centrifuged Coconut Oil is Unlike Any Other

Most commercial virgin coconut oils rely on cold expeller pressing followed by gentle warming fermentation to separate oil from water. While acceptable, even mild fermentation can produce elevated free fatty acid (FFA) levels and slight fermentation aromas.

Our raw centrifuged method involves spinning freshly pressed organic coconut cream through our series of chilled tubular centrifuges at 12,000 RPM. This pure mechanical separation occurs in less than 3 minutes at 28°C. Because no fermentation takes place, the oil maintains a near-zero FFA level (<0.05%), zero toasted flavor, and an extraordinary purity index.
