Pure Extraction From Fresh Kernel to Sealed Glass

COCONEST Extra Virgin Coconut Oil represents the apex of organic botanical processing. Unlike generic copra oil which is extracted from dirty, moldy dried coconut meats and then chemically bleached, our oil is pressed exclusively from freshly de-husked, certified organic coastal coconuts.

Within four hours of cracking, the fresh white coconut meat is cold-grated and gently expeller pressed. Using continuous centrifugal separation, the water, milk solids, and crystalline oil are isolated without any chemical catalysts or artificial heating. The resulting virgin oil possesses a smooth melting texture at 24°C, a subtle fresh coconut aroma, and an unmistakable purity that leaves no oily film.
