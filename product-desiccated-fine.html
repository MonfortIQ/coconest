Pure Ceylon Organic Shredded Coconut

Unlike conventional desiccated coconut that is bleached using sulfur dioxide gas (E220) to enforce an unnatural synthetic whiteness, COCONEST desiccated coconut is 100% untreated and naturally cream-white.

We source directly from heritage coconut orchards in the North Western province of Sri Lanka. Each coconut is pared by hand to remove the brown testa, washed in filtered spring water, finely shredded, and gently hot-air dried below 60°C to preserve natural fats and digestive dietary fiber.
