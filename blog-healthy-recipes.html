5 Chef Superfood Bowls Featuring Crispy Coconut Chips

Aria Vance Culinary Editor • Published Sep 20, 2026
7 min read

A truly nourishing breakfast bowl needs textural equilibrium: creamy velvet bases balanced by an irresistible, satisfying crunch. Our oven-toasted sea salt coconut chips deliver exactly that—without refined sugar, gluten, or hydrogenated seed oils.

Recipe 1: The Coastal Acai & Golden Salt Crunch

Base: Blend 100g frozen unsweetened acai pulp with 1/2 frozen banana, 1 tablespoon of COCONEST virgin coconut oil, and a splash of coconut milk.

Toppings: A generous handful of COCONEST Golden Sea Salt Coconut Chips, fresh wild blueberries, hemp hearts, and a drizzle of raw forest honey. The pink mineral salt cuts through the berry tartness for an unforgettable balance.

Recipe 2: Dark Cacao & Espresso Ketogenic Whip

Base: Whip chilled full-fat organic coconut cream with 1 shot of cooled espresso and 1 tablespoon raw cacao powder.

Toppings: Dark Cacao Coconut Chips, cacao nibs, and toasted crushed hazelnuts. Contains less than 4g net carbs with sustained ketone energy lasting well past midday.
