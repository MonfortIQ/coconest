Radical Transparency

From Coastal Palm to Pure Bottle

Trace the complete 8-stage journey of our certified organic coconut harvest. Every drop of COCONEST virgin coconut oil is accounted for with uncompromising ethics, zero animal labor, and unheated centrifugal extraction.

1
Stage 01

Coconut Farm Origins

Our palms thrive in Sri Lanka’s legendary "Coconut Triangle" (Negombo, Kurunegala, Chilaw). Deep alluvial soils, regular tropical monsoons, and ocean salt-mist create dense kernels bursting with medium-chain fatty acids.

2
Stage 02

Responsible & Ethical Harvesting

We enforce a strict 100% human-ethical harvesting standard. Highly skilled local climbers handpick coconuts at exact ripeness using traditional bamboo tools. We strictly condemn and never use monkey labor.

3
Stage 03

Rigorous Nut Selection

Only fully mature, 11-to-12-month-old coconuts qualify. Each nut is tested by weight and acoustic slosh sound. Any cracked, under-ripe, or sprouted coconuts are immediately diverted to compost.

4
Stage 04

Fresh Processing in 4 Hours

Coconuts are de-husked, cracked, and manually pared to remove the brown testa within 4 hours. The pristine white coconut kernel is washed three times in multi-stage UV-sterilized mountain spring water.

5
Stage 05

Chilled Centrifugal Extraction

Fresh kernel is grated and expeller-pressed under chilled cooling below 38°C. The fresh emulsion is spun in high-speed centrifuges (12,000 RPM) to isolate the crystalline pure virgin oil with zero heating or chemicals.

6
Stage 06

Laboratory Purity Certification

Every extraction batch is tested in an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory. Parameters analyzed include free fatty acid (<0.04%), peroxide value (<0.20 meq/kg), moisture (<0.03%), and complete heavy metal screens.

7
Stage 07

Eco-Friendly & UV-Proof Packaging

Oil is sealed in inert amber glass bottles to block photo-oxidation, while desiccated flakes and chips are packed in certified industrially compostable kraft pouches with non-toxic vegetable-based ink labels.

8
Stage 08

Farm to Customer Delivery

Shipped in temperature-monitored, carbon-neutral freight directly from Sri Lanka to international distribution hubs and straight to your kitchen door, preserving peak vitality and fresh garden sweetness.
