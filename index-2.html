Brand Philosophy

We Believe in Nature Left Entirely Untamed.

"The commercial food system trades vitality for shelf stability. We refuse that bargain. Every drop of our virgin oil is living nutrition, untouched by fire, acid, or synthetic refinement."

Our groves in Western Sri Lanka follow ancient moon-cycle harvesting. We work with nature’s natural rhythm, yielding oils rich in bioactive polyphenols and gentle floral sweetness.

Biodynamic Agroforestry

We never practice monoculture. Our coconut palms grow alongside cacao, black pepper, and neem trees to create natural pest resilience.

Living Micro-Nutrients

Because we do not apply heat, fragile vitamins E and K, plant sterols, and healthy lauric acids remain in their biological native state.

Ethical Direct Stewardship

We pay our farm cooperative partners 40% above fair-trade baseline rates and fund regional schooling and clean rainwater wells.

Zero-Leach UV Glass

Plastic containers leach micro-phthalates into fatty acids. We bottle exclusively in European pharmaceutical-grade amber glass.

The Farm-to-Bottle Sourcing Journey

Explore our transparent supply chain rooted in ancient Sri Lankan coastal groves where every palm is treated as a sovereign organism.

1. Coastal Terroir

Grown in saline coastal soils rich in potassium and ocean mist minerals.

2. Moon Harvest

Gathered by multi-generational climbers using bamboo poles at peak oil content.

3. Chilled Press

Expeller extraction at controlled ambient temperature below 38°C.

4. Glass Sealing

Capped under inert nitrogen in dark amber jars to prevent photo-oxidation.

Planetary Promise
