The COCONEST Standard

Why Pure Food Lovers Worldwide Trust COCONEST

Commercial coconut oils are frequently refined, bleached, and deodorized using harsh chemicals. At COCONEST, we protect nature's purest elixir with an uncompromised artisan cold process.

  • Ultra-Fresh 4-Hour Extraction

    Coconuts are cracked and centrifuge-pressed within 4 hours of tree drop to prevent enzymatic breakdown and acidity rise.

  • Zero Hexane, Bleach, or Deodorizers

    Never exposed to artificial solvents, refining clays, or high heat. You get the authentic velvety aroma of fresh tropical coconut.

  • Regenerative Agroforestry

    Our palms thrive in biodiverse ecosystems alongside vanilla, cinnamon, and bananas, regenerating soil biology and pollinator health.

  • Independent 3rd-Party Lab Verified

    Every batch is tested for heavy metals, moisture (<0.05%), and peroxide value to guarantee supreme therapeutic grade purity.

Traceable Farm-To-Bottle

The Artisan Cold Extraction Journey

Discover how gentle traditional harvesting and state-of-the-art cold centrifugal separation yield the world's most delicate virgin coconut oil.

Step 01
Tree-Ripened Selection

Handpicked mature coconuts (11-12 months) packed with rich organic kernel and water.

Step 02
Fresh Cold Pressing

Kernel grated and expeller-pressed under chilled water cooling below 38°C.

Step 03
Centrifugal Separation

High-speed centrifuge isolates pure crystalline virgin oil from fresh coconut milk.

Step 04
Glass Bottling

Sealed in inert amber and clear glass jars to preserve antioxidants and lauric acid.
