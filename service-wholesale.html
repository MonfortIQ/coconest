Guaranteed Continuity, Farm-Direct Pricing

Wholesale buyers often struggle with inconsistent fat content, sulfur residue, and unpredictable moisture levels in commodity coconut markets. COCONEST eliminates this volatility through vertically integrated cooperative processing.

By controlling harvest cycles and operating our own state-of-the-art cold-milling and centrifugal separation lines in Negombo, Sri Lanka, we provide uninterrupted year-round supply with consistent organoleptic and microbiological specifications.
