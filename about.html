Heritage & Ethics

Pure Nature. Pure Goodness.

Founded in 2014, COCONEST was born from a singular commitment: to restore the purity of the coconut tree to everyday wellness, free from chemicals, industrial high heat, and artificial exploitation.

10+ Years of Purity
Over 40,000 Regenerated Palms
Why We Started

Reclaiming the Elixir from Commercial Refinement

A decade ago, our founders walked into modern grocery stores and discovered that nearly 90% of so-called "coconut oils" were manufactured from dirty copra—dried coconut meats often contaminated with mold during sun drying, requiring heavy industrial refining, bleaching with bentonite clay, and deodorization under chemical steam (RBD).

We knew there was a better way. In the coastal agricultural lands of Negombo, Sri Lanka, traditional families had been extracting living coconut oil for centuries using chilled expellers and hand filtration. We built COCONEST to bring this authentic, unadulterated cold craft to conscious homes worldwide.

100% Direct Estate Trade
40% Above Fair-Trade Pay
Zero Chemical Solvents
Solar Powered Pressing

Our Mission

To deliver the planet's purest, nutrient-dense organic coconut provisions while actively regenerating the coastal ecosystems where our palms grow and ensuring dignified prosperity for every harvesting family in our farming network.

Our Vision

A world where conscious nutrition and sustainable agriculture operate in closed-loop harmony. We envision an uncompromised global supply chain where single-ingredient purity replaces industrial artificial additives in every pantry and vanity.

The Journey

A Decade of Natural Milestones

2014
The First Cold Press

Founded with 15 heirloom family estates in Negombo, harvesting our first batch of centrifuge-separated virgin oil.

2018

Achieved full global organic accreditation across our entire multi-estate agroforestry cooperative network.

2026
Global Pure Living Brand

Now proudly delivering certified virgin coconut provisions to over 38 countries across culinary, wellness, and wholesale sectors.
