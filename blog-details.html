Sustainable Agroforestry: Protecting Tropical Ecosystems

Camille Chen VP Global Ethics • Published Sep 11, 2026
6 min read

Agriculture today accounts for nearly 24% of global greenhouse emissions. However, when managed in true agroforestry systems, tree crops like coconuts are potent net-negative carbon sinks that build permanent living soil organic matter.

At COCONEST, we believe every business must give back more to the soil than it extracts. Our multi-tiered canopy agroforestry practices restore degraded lands and protect native biodiversity.

1. Closed-Loop Biomass Utilization

Every single portion of the coconut is put to use: coir fiber for organic horticulture, activated charcoal from shells for water purification, and nutrient-rich biomass return to fertilize the grove floor naturally.

2. 100% Rooftop Solar Powered Operations

Our expeller press and cold centrifugal milling lines are powered completely by on-site solar arrays, feeding clean excess energy back to the local grid.
